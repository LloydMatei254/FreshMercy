Who We Are

A Gospel-Centered Community

Fresh Mercy is a gospel-rooted ministry built on one profound truth — God's mercy is renewed every single morning. No matter where you've been or what you've carried, His compassions never fail.

We exist to point every weary heart back to the Father who runs toward the prodigal, who lifts the broken, and whose love is steadfast and unending. This is not religion — this is relationship.

Our Pillars
What We Stand On

Our Pillars

Grace Alone

Salvation is not earned — it is freely given. We proclaim the grace of God that appears to all people, offering redemption through Christ alone.

The Living Word

Scripture is our foundation and our daily bread. We dig into God's Word to be transformed, not just informed — letting it renew our minds and shape our lives.

Prayer & Surrender

We believe prayer is not a last resort — it is our first response. In surrender, we find strength. In seeking Him, we are found.

Authentic Community

Mercy is best shared, not stored. We build honest, life-giving community where the broken are welcomed, the weary find rest, and no one walks alone.

Daily Renewal

New morning, new mercy. We teach the rhythm of daily returning to God — through devotion, repentance, and the continual renewal of His spirit within us.

Gospel Mission

The Great Commission is not optional. We go — into our homes, our neighborhoods, and to the ends of the earth — carrying the good news of Jesus Christ.
